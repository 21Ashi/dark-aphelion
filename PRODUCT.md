# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Two audiences, weighted equally: hiring managers and engineering leads deciding whether to talk to Abood about a full-time role, and founders or agencies deciding whether to commission an app from him. Both arrive on a laptop or a phone, usually from a link in a CV, a GitHub profile or a message, and give the site a few minutes at most. They want to see real shipped work, what he personally built, and the judgement behind it.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio for Abood Ashi, a full-stack and Flutter developer. It exists to make a visitor remember him and then act: open a case study, then get in touch. Success is a visitor who reaches a case study, understands the problem, the solution and his part in it, and opens the contact links.

## Brand Personality

Cinematic, exacting, quietly confident. A dark stage lit by a crimson star; motion that feels directed rather than decorative. Copy is plain and specific: what was built, for whom, and why it was hard. Never boastful, never vague.

## Anti-references

- Conventional portfolio grids: three cards with an image, a title and a button.
- Template developer portfolios with skill bars, logo walls and "passionate developer" copy.
- Uniform fade-up-on-scroll for every section.
- Inflated claims: invented clients, statistics or achievements.

## Design Principles

1. The work is the exhibit. Each project gets its own room with its own light; the site's identity is the building.
2. Show the judgement, not just the screenshot: problem, solution and personal contribution, in plain words.
3. Motion directs attention. Every transition moves the visitor from one idea to the next; nothing moves for its own sake.
4. Truth over polish. Only facts traceable to the project's own docs and history; missing information stays an honest gap.
5. The phone is a first-class stage, not a fallback.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA contrast for text. Full keyboard access, including the case-study dialog. `prefers-reduced-motion` gets native scrolling, no pinning or scrubbing, and content shown without entrance motion. No hover-only information on touch devices.
